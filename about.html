The Story Behind Our Name

How a family's tribute to one woman's compassion became a trust serving thousands across Sindh.

Every family carries a story of someone whose kindness outlived them. For the Laghari family of Sindh, that story belongs to Bilqees Razia Laghari — a woman remembered for turning her own home into a refuge for the sick, the hungry and the forgotten.

In her memory, and under the guidance of her family, the Bilqees Razia Laghari Welfare Trust was established to formalise what she had always done informally: show up for people with no one else to turn to.

From modest beginnings — a handful of ration bags and a single medical camp — the Trust has grown into a network of 13 active program categories spanning education, health, hygiene, community relief and digital empowerment, reaching families across dozens of villages in Sindh.

Our Mission

To deliver dignified, sustainable support in education, health and community welfare to the underserved communities of Sindh — reaching the families that formal systems overlook.

Our Vision

A Sindh where no child is out of school, no patient is out of reach of care, and no village is left behind by the digital future — the very future Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari calls Digital Sindh.
