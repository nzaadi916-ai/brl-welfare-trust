Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Strategy

The principles driving our daily operations and community decisions.

Our Vision

A Sindh where no child is out of school, no patient is out of reach of care, and no village is left behind by the digital future — the very future Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari calls Digital Sindh.

Our Mission

To deliver dignified, sustainable support in education, health and community welfare to the underserved communities of Sindh — reaching the families that formal systems overlook.

Our Strategy

Growing village by village — from 40+ communities today toward every flood-affected corner of Sindh, with Digital Sindh's computer-literacy program as the next frontier.

A Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari Initiative

Combined Marriages

In the aftermath of the devastating 2022 floods, many young women across flood-hit Sindh were left waiting at home — their weddings postponed indefinitely as families lost everything they had.

Under the leadership of Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari, the Trust launched the Combined Marriages initiative to send them off the way every bride deserves: with dignity, and with a home of their own to start in.
