Why We Built This Trust

A Personal Letter

From the desk of Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari

Trust Coordinator

I did not set out to build an organisation. I set out to keep a promise — that the compassion Bilqees Razia Laghari showed to everyone around her would not end with her.

She never turned away a family in need, never asked what they could offer in return. That is the only standard I have ever tried to hold this Trust to: show up for people the rest of the world has stopped showing up for.

Every ration bag, every eye camp, every computer we place in a village classroom is the same promise, kept a little further down the road. We are not finished — not by a long way — but every village we reach is proof that the promise still holds.

Thank you for reading this far, and for considering standing with us.

Ahmad Sultan
Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari
Coordinator, Bilqees Razia Laghari Welfare Trust
