A Pillar of the Trust

As Coordinator of the Bilqees Razia Laghari Welfare Trust, Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari has been one of the steadiest pillars behind the Trust's grassroots work — connecting field teams, health camps and education programs across dozens of villages in Sindh.

Beyond his coordination role, Dr. Ahmad Sultan Laghari carries a deeply personal mission: to see Sindh transformed into a digitally empowered province where technology closes the gap left by geography — starting with the villages where a computer has never entered a classroom.

He calls this dream simply — Digital Sindh.

“My dream is a Digital Sindh — where every village has connectivity, and every child has access to knowledge.”

Digital Sindh in Action

Digital Sindh starts with a simple, concrete step: putting a working computer lab in front of students who have never touched one before.

The Trust has donated computer labs and run hands-on digital-skills classes in village schools across Sindh — teaching children to type, navigate, and use a computer confidently, often for the very first time.
